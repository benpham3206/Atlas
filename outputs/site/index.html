Atlas

Proof-closed knowledge: a structured encyclopedia where nothing becomes canon without evidence and a hash-chained audit trail. Wikidata's structure, Britannica's authority, Wikipedia's openness — made honest by proof.

01 — Knowledge tier

Entities, statements, sources, and links — structured graph first, derived prose second. The proof-closed member beside Wikipedia, Wikidata, Britannica, and Grokipedia.

02 — Governed staff

Authoring by scoped agent hires — Librarian, Researcher, Verifier, Curator — under lifecycle gates and Board approval, not an anonymous crowd or opaque single model.

03 — Proof-closed

Every operational statement traces to source + evidence. Candidate records promote only after review; the audit log is hash-chained and verifiable.
